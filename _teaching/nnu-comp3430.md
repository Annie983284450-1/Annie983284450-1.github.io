* Teach two linked undergraduate courses: COMP3430 Operating Systems (lecture, 3 credits) and COMP3430L Operating Systems Lab (1 credit).
* The lecture follows *Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces* (OSTEP). The lab runs on xv6, the teaching kernel from MIT.
* Write and publish a free [self-study guide](https://annie983284450-1.github.io/NNU-CS3430-selfstudyguide/) for the course: one guide per week, with worked examples, real kernel code and outputs captured on a real machine.
* Build follow-along [OS labs on xv6](https://annie983284450-1.github.io/NNU-CS3430-selfstudyguide/labs/) (util, syscall, pgtbl). Each step links back to the theory it puts into practice.
