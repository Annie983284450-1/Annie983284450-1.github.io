Adjunct Professor

COMP3430 Operating Systems and COMP3430L Operating Systems Lab

I teach two linked undergraduate courses: the Operating Systems lecture and its lab. The lecture follows Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces. In the lab, students work on xv6, the teaching kernel from MIT, and change a real kernel step by step. I also write a free self-study guide for both, one guide per week. Every program and output on that site is a real file, captured on a real machine.
